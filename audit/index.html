FULL RESEARCH AUDIT / 17 OCT 2026

Full research audit.

Corrections, evidence upgrades and a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction account of what is still missing.

This is an editorial, documentary and numerical audit of the full published corpus. It is not a completed investigation of every voter case. No national wrongful-exclusion total is established.

Corrections and improvements

A01 · Older Act compilation was presented without a version warning

Relabelled historical; current-law consolidation remains an explicit gap. RPA-1950

A02 · Uttarakhand narrative over-emphasised a historical printed-date anomaly

Added later operational source and final-roll reconciliation; no claim of actual pre-draft notices. UK-DRAFT-JUL14

A03 · Tripura May dates lacked a visible later-schedule update

Added public-broadcaster account, kept signed replacement pending. TRIPURA-REVISED-AIR

A04 · State HTML, JSON, source lists and chronology had diverged

Rebuilt data-backed sections from one research dataset; added regression checks.

A05 · Different applicant populations risk misleading restoration rates

Kept submissions, accepted claims, unique people and published entries separate. MH-CEO-OCT09

A06 · Reference-file coverage risked being mistaken for completed investigations

Added a 36-jurisdiction coverage matrix with explicit case-audit limits. ECI-SSR-OCT15

Primary-source gaps filled

  • West Bengal January and February orders now inspected with case and paragraph locators.
  • Uttarakhand operational and final-publication sources located; 13 district equations and state totals reconciled.
  • Standard Form 6 declaration inspected. The online SIR version remains a separate missing record.
  • Citizenship Act and Form 7 guidance refreshed; statutory-version limitations recorded.

Closure criteria

A gap closes only when its decisive evidence is obtained and examined. An indexed URL, a press statement, an accepted application or a proposed audit cannot replace the underlying record needed for the particular conclusion.

There are 44 source records and 23 tracked claims. Mirrors and repeated references are disclosed. Read the complete gap register.
