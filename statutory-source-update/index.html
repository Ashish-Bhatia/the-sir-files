RESEARCH NOTE / 17 OCTOBER 2026

A newer Act compilation. A clearer version boundary.

The ECI’s July 2025 compilation fills a source gap. It does not certify every legal change through October 2026.

A better starting point

The ECI publishes a 33-page Representation of the People Act, 1950 compilation labelled 22 July 2025. Its amendment list includes Act 49 of 2021. Section 14(b) includes four qualifying dates. Sections 23(4)–(6) include the Aadhaar-related provisions. ECI compilation

Why the date matters

Our earlier linked compilation omitted later amendments and remains labelled historical. Finding a newer file improves the baseline; it does not establish that nothing changed after its stated date. Full currency requires checking later Acts, commencement notifications and the applicable rule versions.

Read the declaration issue precisely

Section 31 concerns a written false statement with the specified knowledge or belief. It is not a rule that every returning applicant automatically commits an offence. Nor does this source settle the wording or operation of a later online form. Those are different evidence questions. Section 31, printed page 23

What we verified

The indexed PDF text was inspected. Direct byte retrieval returned HTTP 406, so no local file hash or complete visual inspection is claimed. This finding partly fills the statutory-source gap. It does not close it.
