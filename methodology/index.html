SIR BASICS

What the figures mean.

Similar-looking numbers describe different steps in the process.

Draft omissions

A name absent from the draft roll is not automatically a final exclusion. Claims, objections and decisions follow the draft stage.

Applications and accepted claims

A submitted application records a request. An accepted claim records a decision. The corresponding entry in a published roll or supplement establishes the recorded registration.

Software flags and deletions

A discrepancy flag identifies a record for checking. It does not, on its own, establish a deletion or a correct eligibility decision.

Net changes in roll size

The difference between two totals includes additions and removals. It is not a count of people wrongly excluded. Changes must be compared at the same legal stage and with matching dates.

Court directions and outcomes

A court order establishes what was directed or decided. Whether an official followed it, and what happened to a particular voter, are separate factual questions.
