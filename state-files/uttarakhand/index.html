ARCHIVE STATUSActive

Last desk review: 15 October 2026

A four-day question in the original timetable

On page 30 of the 14 May ECI order, Uttarakhand’s draft is dated 14 July, while a notice/disposal window begins on 10 July. The dates do not align with the ordinary understanding of post-draft discrepancy notices. There may be a clerical explanation or an intentionally broader preparatory window. The original document alone cannot decide which.

What the original ECI schedule said

Relevant historical reference01 Jan 2003
Original draft target14 Jul 2026
Original final publication target15 Sep 2026

These are original Annexures V and VI, not proof that a later amended schedule did not supersede them.

Read the whole order

The same ECI order says notices for unmatched or discrepant cases are to be issued after publication of the draft. The apparent tension therefore deserves clarification from the operative state circulars.

The decisive files

The public timetable should be compared with actual service dates, types of notices and the responsible ERO’s statutory authority. A contradictory printed date does not establish that any elector was denied a hearing.

A 2003 reference

The historical matching date is 1 January 2003. It is evidence used in processing, not a substitute for present-day eligibility.

What this file still needs

  1. Was a corrigendum issued for the notice/disposal window?
  2. Were any statutory notices served before the draft?
  3. Was the public given an updated schedule by the CEO and DEOs?
