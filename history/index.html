Continuation: the next revision creates a new chronological boundary.
HISTORY / THE STORY SO FAR

Before a name can disappear, there is a process.

Intensive revisions are not new to India. The 2025–26 programme, however, revived a difficult question: how should a democratic state check its voter lists without turning an administrative exercise into an avoidable barrier to the vote?

A law older than this dispute

The Representation of the People Act, 1950, and the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960, explain what an electoral roll is meant to record, who can seek inclusion and how an objection should be decided. The Election Commission has carried out intensive revisions at different times and in different parts of the country. A historical roll can be useful evidence of an earlier registration—but it is not a permanent test of citizenship in 2026.

The 14 May 2026 order itself lists different reference years for different States and Union Territories: 2002 in some places, 2003 in others, 2005 or 2006 elsewhere. That difference matters when officers and software compare two eras of the register.

2025: Bihar first

ECI began with Bihar in June 2025. The draft roll issued in August was smaller than the pre-SIR electorate by roughly 65 lakh entries. Public debate often treated that difference as permanent deletion; the correct question was always what later happened through claims, objections and final statutory decisions.

By September the final roll had recovered from the draft. The question of whether individual exclusions were correct did not disappear merely because the overall process was completed.

2026: the constitutional answer

On 27 May 2026, the Supreme Court upheld the legality of Bihar’s SIR. It accepted the Commission’s authority to carry out a special revision, while distinguishing the limited citizenship inquiry relevant to electoral eligibility from the decision that legally determines citizenship under the Citizenship Act.

The Court settled the framework. The case files still have to settle the facts.

Nineteen jurisdictions in a new phase

The Commission’s 14 May order directed Phase III in 16 States and 3 Union Territories. Annexure VI staggered original draft and final dates. It also expressly said that supporting documents were not to be collected during the enumeration phase, while post-draft notices could be used for unmatched and discrepant historical links.

The wider programme created questions that cannot be answered with Bihar’s data alone: differing historical reference years, revised state schedules, enrolment-path disputes and electronic processing rules.

A system tries to correct itself

By September and October, ECI had announced changes to how documents would be collected, a review of ECINet and a post-SIR drive to revisit people on the old rolls who were absent from the new. Those directives are official actions. Their success is another question. We need final counts of people reached, applications approved and decisions actually reflected in the published register.

Chronology and review trail

1950

The electoral register acquires a statutory foundation

The Representation of the People Act governs qualifications, additions, corrections and appeals.

1960

Registration of Electors Rules

Procedure for drafting, claims, hearings, amendments and intensive revision.

2002–06

State-specific intensive-revision reference rolls

The reference years differ by jurisdiction. They are historical evidence, not a certificate of citizenship.

24 Jun 2025

ECI orders SIR, starting with Bihar

The decision invokes Article 324 and the election laws; scrutiny turns to the process and access to remedies.

01 Aug 2025

Bihar draft roll appears

Approximately 65 lakh pre-SIR entries are absent from the provisional draft.

30 Sep 2025

Bihar final roll published

Around 7.42 crore voters; additions and further removals mean draft absence does not map one-to-one to final exclusion.

27 Oct 2025

A subsequent multi-jurisdiction phase

Different jurisdictions follow their own timetables and publication rules.

19 Jan–20 Feb 2026

West Bengal court directions

Accessible discrepancy notices and an exceptional framework for unresolved cases.

21 Feb 2026

Goa publishes final roll

Its signed report permits exact draft-to-final reconciliation.

14 May 2026

Phase III covers 19 States/UTs

The May order contains distinct draft and final targets, historical years and the no-documents-during-enumeration rule.

27 May 2026

Supreme Court judgment on Bihar SIR

The broad process is upheld; citizenship determinations remain legally bounded.

14 Jul 2026

Uttarakhand operational bulletin

Later source clarifies the operational notice window; historical May dates remain separately identified.

10 Sep 2026

Tripura revised timetable reported

Public broadcaster reports a later schedule; signed replacement not yet obtained.

26 Sep 2026

New ECI safeguards and review promise

Doorstep document collection and an announced independent-expert review of ECINet.

01 Oct 2026

ECI instructs a post-SIR revisit

BLOs are directed to compare old/current rolls, visit absent electors and assist eligible applicants.

03 Oct 2026

Uttarakhand final publication reported

Official state report and district annexure now reconciled.

Goa announces special enrolment camps
