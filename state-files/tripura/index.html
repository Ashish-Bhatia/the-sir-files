Last desk review: 15 October 2026

The correction file

This case is about accountability in research as much as election administration. An earlier edition of The SIR Files suggested that ECI had published an impossible claims period. Inspection of the complete 30-page order showed the end date was 20 November, not 20 October. The incorrect assertion was withdrawn.

What the original ECI schedule said

Relevant historical reference01 Jan 2005
Original draft target21 Oct 2026
Original final publication target23 Dec 2026

These are original Annexures V and VI, not proof that a later amended schedule did not supersede them.

What the controlling order says

Annexure VI of the 14 May 2026 order provides an internally consistent schedule. Any later amendment should be examined separately.

Why the correction stays public

A research archive should preserve its corrections. The evidence label was changed when the original document was examined, rather than concealing the old error.

What remains worth investigating

The further question is whether actual revision steps, notifications, Form 6 submissions and decision registers followed the operative schedule.

Public correction · 15 October 2026: An earlier version of this research incorrectly claimed the original ECI claims window closed before it opened. Inspection of the complete signed order confirms the interval 21 October–20 November 2026. The error is withdrawn and preserved here for transparency.

What this file still needs

  1. Were any subsequent schedule amendments issued for Tripura?
  2. Are district and constituency notices publicly archived?
  3. Do the actual case files meet the post-draft notice standard?
