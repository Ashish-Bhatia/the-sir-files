CASE STUDIES / CHECKING THE NUMBERSGoa

The totals balance. The individual decisions remain open.

Goa’s signed report gives this investigation a complete draft-to-final equation. It also shows why an accounting result is only one part of an outcome audit.

THE QUESTION

What does a correct reconciliation prove?

How the record developed

  1. 01

    Start with the same populations

    Keep the pre-SIR, draft and final rolls distinct. Each is a stock at a different stage, not a count of people wrongly removed.

  2. 02

    Reconcile the movement

    The draft-to-final equation includes accepted additions and two removal categories. The figures balance against the final count.

  3. 03

    Return to the people affected

    The separate 97-person discussion concerns applications and reported acceptances. Individual orders and matched supplements are still needed to establish outcomes.

WHAT THE RECORD SUPPORTS

What is established

The official draft-to-final figures reconcile arithmetically.

THE LIMIT

Where the evidence stops

A balanced total does not establish the legality of every eligibility decision.

What remains unresolved

Individual decision files, reasons, appeal outcomes and linked roll entries.

A documentary finding and an individual outcome are different. We keep the missing evidence visible.
