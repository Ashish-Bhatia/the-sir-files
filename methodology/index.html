THE EDITORIAL DESK / CORRECTION LOG

Evidence should change
the story.

This is an independent research publication, not an allegation generator or a government portal. Its purpose is to say what primary documents prove, what competing explanations remain possible, and what could make our present conclusion wrong.

Our evidence hierarchy

Judicial finding: a reasoned and authenticated decision, interpreted within the exact issue it decided. Official statement: evidence of the institution’s representation; case-level implementation must be checked separately. Administrative dataset: a quantified record with a clear date and denominator. Secondary report: reporting that can direct an inquiry but may not substitute for the original record. Open question: an issue where decisive evidence has not yet been obtained.

How numbers are used

We do not add provisional draft non-inclusions to final deletions, count transfers as unregistered people, or treat a filed Form 6 as an accepted inclusion. Every figure should say which population it measures, for what date and how it was generated. A nationally comparable series must use the same stage, or explicitly disclose where it cannot.

What neutrality means in practice

ECI explanations are examined rather than presumed correct; opposition allegations are examined rather than presumed false. A procedural error does not independently establish deliberate electoral fraud. Conversely, a Supreme Court decision upholding the overall framework cannot resolve every later local processing error.

What this version can and cannot do

The site provides real, individually addressable articles; a searchable source library; a filterable state directory; a portable data export; and working share links. It is a static publication and is not connected to the ECI’s live data, Supreme Court docket, a background research agent or an editorial content-management server. New verified evidence should be reviewed and published as dated revisions, not quietly inserted without provenance.
