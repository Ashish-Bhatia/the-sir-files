SIR stands for Special Intensive Revision: preparing electoral rolls afresh. The investigation asks what the records establish about the process and its effects.

Why the steps matter

A draft omission, an application, an accepted claim and a published entry describe different stages. A court ruling also has a defined scope. Read these stages separately before treating them as an outcome for a person.

A consistent reading path

Every case starts with the question and the sequence of events. It then separates what is established from what remains unresolved. The supporting chapters follow in order, with the source record alongside them.

Understand the coverage

Eight jurisdictions have developed analysis. Twenty-eight remain reference files. Grouping the material does not turn a reference file into a completed case audit.
