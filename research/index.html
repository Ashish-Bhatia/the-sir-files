RESEARCH REVIEW / 17 OCTOBER 2026

What the record establishes.

A dated assessment of SIR authority, exclusion risks, disputed forms and the evidence still required. This review carries forward the earlier archive and identifies fresh checks.

SIR means Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls. This publication covers India’s 2025–26 exercise. It contains 36 jurisdiction files, including 8 developed analyses and 28 reference files. It is an initial investigative edition, not a completed national forensic audit.

1. A lawful framework and a contested implementation

The 27 May judgment upheld Bihar’s SIR. It also preserved the distinction between electoral eligibility inquiry and a final citizenship determination. Its operative directions address referral of specified citizenship cases and judicial review for wrongful absence, death, shifting or duplicate classifications. These are distinct questions from the validity of a later form or the implementation of a specific ERO decision. Judgment, paragraphs 186–187

2. The national total needs a defined population

The research archive does not establish a national count of wrongful exclusions. Such a measure requires the same revision stage and cutoff date across states, verified eligibility and person-level outcomes. Gross omissions, net roll decreases and accepted applications measure different events. Neither a large reduction nor a favourable judicial ruling resolves all individual cases.

Use three separate series: pre-to-draft changes, draft-to-final movements, and post-final restoration. Report pending claims and appeals alongside outcomes. Never classify people as non-citizens from names, language, neighbourhoods or failed historical links.

3. Goa: accounting is possible, legality still needs files

The official final-publication note supplies a reproducible reconciliation: 1,084,992 + 12,166 − 3,812 − 35,780 = 1,057,566. The pre-SIR total was 1,185,034. This gives a net pre-to-final decrease of 127,468, or 10.76%, and a draft-to-final decrease of 27,426, or 2.53%. These percentages use different denominators. CEO Goa, 21 February, pages 1–3

The narrow 97-person controversy must be audited separately. PTI reported the CEO’s statement that 88 applications had been accepted. That is a later institutional account, not this publication’s inspection of 88 restored roll entries. PTI-GOA-OCT02

4. Form 6: identify the exact version

The Commission’s September statement asserted judicial backing for the SIR declaration. Court reporting on 12 October instead describes no adjudication on the revised form. The proper status is unresolved here: neither approval nor invalidation of the precise revised version is established by this archive’s signed October records. NAG-SEP26 LB-FORM6-OCT12 IE-FORM6-OCT12

Collect the statutory form, each attached declaration, online screenshots with capture dates, implementation circulars, approval files, and the signed court record. Compare mandatory fields, eligibility assertions and consequences for non-completion. A statement about documentation authority is not enough to authenticate the version challenged in October.

5. Form 7: an evidence gap is closed

The signed 9 October order is now available. Its application limits and safeguards are documented in our Form 7 file. The source question has moved to implementation: actual objections, notices, decisions and sanctions.

6. Governance and ECINet need separate tests

The official September account announces expert review and says IT oversight was not withdrawn. This records the Commission’s position. It does not provide the expert report, role-permission history or raw approval minutes. NAG-SEP26

A 15 October press report describes a fresh petition and notice. Allegations in a petition and a notice seeking a response establish no wrongdoing by themselves. The signed order and full pleadings remain required before presenting their precise scope as judicially verified. TOI-NOTICE-OCT15

Publication decisions

  • Retain the earlier Tripura correction. The original claims window was 21 October–20 November. Historical schedules remain labelled historical.
  • Upgrade the Form 7 source from pending to inspected. Do not claim implementation is proven.
  • Keep the Goa cohort, Form 6 litigation, governance records and national wrongful-exclusion count open.
  • Publish no individual voter identity documents or searchable collection of personal details.

Next evidence, ranked by impact

  1. Goa: signed determinations and supplement reconciliation for the disputed 97-person cohort.
  2. Form 6: authenticated October orders, exact challenged declaration, approval and version history.
  3. ECINet: independent review report, access-control history and decision-to-publication reconciliation.
  4. National: dated state totals with common definitions and verified restoration outcomes.
  5. Form 7: implementation returns and samples of accepted and rejected objections after 9 October.

These are research priorities. No RTI, court filing or communication is represented as submitted.
