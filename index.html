Arunachal Pradesh

Chandigarh

Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu

Gujarat

Haryana

04 / HOW WE GOT HERE

The story didn’t
start yesterday.

Orders, court rulings, changing timetables. Open a moment to see what changed.

Read the full history →
1950The electoral register acquires a statutory foundation+

The Representation of the People Act governs qualifications, additions, corrections and appeals.

1960Registration of Electors Rules+

Procedure for drafting, claims, hearings, amendments and intensive revision.

2002–06State-specific intensive-revision reference rolls+

The reference years differ by jurisdiction. They are historical evidence, not a certificate of citizenship.

24 Jun 2025ECI orders SIR, starting with Bihar+

The decision invokes Article 324 and the election laws; scrutiny turns to the process and access to remedies.

01 Aug 2025Bihar draft roll appears+

Approximately 65 lakh pre-SIR entries are absent from the provisional draft.

30 Sep 2025Bihar final roll published+

Around 7.42 crore voters; additions and further removals mean draft absence does not map one-to-one to final exclusion.

27 Oct 2025A subsequent multi-jurisdiction phase+

Different jurisdictions follow their own timetables and publication rules.

19 Jan–20 Feb 2026West Bengal court directions+

Accessible discrepancy notices and an exceptional framework for unresolved cases.

21 Feb 2026Goa publishes final roll+

Its signed report permits exact draft-to-final reconciliation.

14 May 2026Phase III covers 19 States/UTs+

The May order contains distinct draft and final targets, historical years and the no-documents-during-enumeration rule.

27 May 2026Supreme Court judgment on Bihar SIR+

The broad process is upheld; citizenship determinations remain legally bounded.

14 Jul 2026Uttarakhand operational bulletin+

Later source clarifies the operational notice window; historical May dates remain separately identified.

10 Sep 2026Tripura revised timetable reported+

Public broadcaster reports a later schedule; signed replacement not yet obtained.

26 Sep 2026New ECI safeguards and review promise+

Doorstep document collection and an announced independent-expert review of ECINet.

01 Oct 2026ECI instructs a post-SIR revisit+

BLOs are directed to compare old/current rolls, visit absent electors and assist eligible applicants.

03 Oct 2026Uttarakhand final publication reported+

Official state report and district annexure now reconciled.

07 Oct 2026Goa announces special enrolment camps+

State implementation is documented; actual results still need registers and supplements.

09 Oct 2026Form 7 limits issued+

Signed ECI order located and inspected in the 17 October review.

12 Oct 2026Reported Form 6 clarification+

Court reporting says neither approval nor disapproval; signed October order remains to be authenticated.

15 Oct 2026Archive reconciles Goa and corrects Tripura report+

A signed source resolves a previous research error; the audited Goa figures are separated by legal stage.

15 Oct 2026Further petition reported+

Press account reports notice on SIR governance allegations; no merits finding established.

15 Oct 20262027 SSR ordered in 17 jurisdictions+

A separate revision stage; the order defers schedules elsewhere. Later SSR entries must be reconciled with SIR and continuous-update records.

17 Oct 2026GitHub publication review+

Earlier archive recovered, source gaps narrowed and claims register added.
