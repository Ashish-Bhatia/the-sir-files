G08 / Open

G09 / Partly filled

G10 / Open

Announcement remains an official position, not a completed audit.

Required: Full expert report, scope, rule versions, permission and transaction history.

G11 / Open

Commission approval records

G12 / Open

G13 / Open

G14 / Open
