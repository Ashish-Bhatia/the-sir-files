The baseline is a reference, not a verdict

Being unlinked to the January 2002 roll is not proof of non-citizenship or absence of ordinary residence in 2026. The legal question for the ERO remains present eligibility.

The response to a notice must count

The number of people who supplied documents is an activity measure. What matters for inclusion is how many decisions were made, how many were favourable and whether the correct entries appeared in the final roll.

A practical route to accountability

Data should be obtainable by constituency: flagged cases, served notices, responses received, reasons for rejection, successful appeals and corrections.

What the 14 October order records

In W.P.(C) 14955/2026, the Court closed the writ proceedings and allowed the two petitioners to fill Form 6 for processing under law. This records an application route, not a completed restoration or a ruling on every challenged declaration.

The date on the page and the date on the order

The district directory lists the revised schedule under 19 September, but the signed letter itself is dated 19 August. It sets a 31 August draft and earlier September/November targets. Later extensions must be read separately.

Applications received are not names restored

The 17 October CEO note’s indexed text reports 232,514 Form 6 applications during the claims period and 212,400 before draft publication. These are intake counts. Acceptance, unique people and published inclusion are not established by those totals.

The qualifying date in the July bundle

The CEO forwarding letter refers to 1 October, while the attached ECI order’s subject refers to 1 July. The timetable dates match. This is a wording inconsistency, not proof of an incorrect eligibility decision. The later August order uses 1 October.

How Delhi’s timetable changed

The dates below belong to older signed orders. They are not current deadlines. The district website lists the August letter under 19 September; its signed header is dated 19 August. The July bundle also differs internally: the CEO forwarding subject refers to 1 October as qualifying date, while the attached ECI subject says 1 July. The effect on actual decisions is not established.
