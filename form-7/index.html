INDIA / SIR / 2025—2026THE LAW
RESEARCH REVIEW / 17 OCTOBER 2026

The Form 7 evidence file.

The 9 October source is now inspected. Application limits, adjudication and actual removals must still be measured separately.

The signed order

ECI order No.23/Inst/2026-ERS, dated 9 October 2026, sets limits for individual electors in online and offline modes: 10 applications over the claims-and-objections period and five per month during continuous updating. It separately recites BLA submission limits of 50 per day before draft publication and 10 per day afterwards. Individual and BLA limits must not be conflated. Order, paragraphs 3–5

The order requires applicant verification, signatures, scrutiny, and a hearing opportunity for the person objected to before an adverse order in the applicable process. It directs action for knowingly false written declarations under Section 31. It does not establish that any named individual committed an offence. Order, paragraphs 6–8

What the order does not demonstrate

A restriction is an administrative instruction. It is not evidence that every portal enforced it, that all older objections were invalid, or that every accepted objection was correct. Assess conduct against the rules and instructions applicable at its actual date.

A five-stage investigation

Design the sample before seeing outcomes

Stratify by constituency, submission mode, reason, objector volume and decision outcome. Include rejected applications as well as accepted ones. Preserve duplicate applications without counting the same person twice. Record manual overrides and exceptions. Sample selection and exclusions must be reproducible.

For each case, record which instruction was operative, who decided it, whether notice reached the elector, and whether a successful appeal was implemented before the relevant election cutoff. Compare high-volume objectors with ordinary applicants, controlling for constituency and reason. A concentration is a lead for investigation, not proof of coordinated misconduct.

Evidence needed for different conclusions

  • Attempted false objection: authenticated submission plus evidence the claim was knowingly false.
  • Wrongful removal: eligibility evidence, adverse decision, and the affected published entry.
  • Systematic failure: a defined sampling frame and a reproducible pattern beyond isolated cases.
  • Intent or coordination: records connecting actors, decisions and purpose, tested against alternative explanations.

Historical Aland allegations remain a separate comparator. They are not evidence about every 2026 objection. PIB-ALAND
