Form 6: compare the exact version.

The standard declaration is now inspected. The online version, authorising instructions and signed October court record remain separate evidence questions.

17 October update. A Delhi High Court order copy is now inspected. The broader Supreme Court question remains separate. Read the order analysis →

The standard PDF provides a concrete starting point

Declaration (iii) in the official standard Form 6 asserts first-time inclusion and absence from another constituency’s roll. Official form, page 2

The legal question raised by previously registered applicants cannot be answered by relabelling every applicant “new”. Nor does the standard PDF establish what every online SIR screen required on a particular date.

Do not infer the result of the legal challenge

October reporting describes no adjudication on the revised form. The source remains reporting of courtroom remarks. It is not a signed order invalidating the form or approving its precise later implementation. LB-FORM6-OCT12

What a reproducible comparison needs

Preserve the PDF and online version identifier, effective date, instruction authorising each field, conditions for submission, and treatment of someone previously enrolled. Compare the exact declaration text, rather than summaries of its purpose. Do not ask an applicant to make a statement that conflicts with their known registration history; the research task is to locate the applicable lawful instruction and case record.

Legal sources need version control too

The earlier linked Act compilation lacks later amendments. A newer ECI text dated July 2025 is now located. Later amendments, commencement instruments and rule versions remain to be checked. Read the statutory-source update. The page now labels that limitation rather than presenting the text as fully current. RPA-1950

Section 23(3), as reproduced in the linked text, covers amendments, transpositions and deletions under Section 22 as well as inclusion directions during the defined election-period freeze. A general statement about continuous enrolment must be read with that restriction and the operative election dates.
