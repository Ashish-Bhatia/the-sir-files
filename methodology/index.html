About The SIR Files.

What this site covers

This is an independent explanation of India’s Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls. It is not affiliated with the Election Commission or a political party. Events and documents covered here run through 17 October 2026.

How to read the figures

Draft omissions, applications, accepted claims and published registrations describe different stages. We do not combine them into a national count of wrongful exclusions. A court order and the outcome for an individual voter are also different things.

Documents and limits

Official orders, court decisions, reported statements and our calculations are identified separately. A missing document remains missing. The source list records qualifications and access problems.
