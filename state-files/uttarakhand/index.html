Uttarakhand

A later operational bulletin narrows the printed-date question. The October final report supplies a district-level reconciliation.

Draft entries7,133,785
Final entries7,158,949
Final publication reported3 Oct

The May annexure’s 10 July notice start is a historical printed-date discrepancy. The July operational bulletin instead begins that window on 14 July. The final report published on 3 October now gives a stronger basis for analysing aggregate outcomes.

What is established here

SSR scheduled in the 15 October order. ECI-SSR-OCT15

Historical reference: 01 Jan 2003. ECI-2026-05

Original May targetDate
Draft14 Jul 2026
Final15 Sep 2026

Historical targets. This table is not a current timetable.

Narrow the earlier question

The operational bulletin starts the notice/disposal window on 14 July. This does not prove whether any individual notice was served earlier, but it removes any basis to describe the May date alone as proof of actual pre-draft proceedings.

The arithmetic now has a primary source

Draft entries plus admitted claims minus admitted objections reconcile to the reported final roll, both statewide and in all 13 district totals. Admitted claims include transfer-related form categories; they must not be described as all new voters or restorations.

What remains untested

Case files, notice-service evidence, reasons, appeals and publication dates are still required to decide the correctness of individual exclusions.

Evidence still needed

  • Were any notices actually issued before the draft, despite the July operational window?
  • What reasons and hearings supported admitted objections?
  • Which accepted cases concern earlier omissions rather than new eligibility or transfers?
