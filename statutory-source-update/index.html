The Act and its date.

The ECI compilation

The ECI publishes a Representation of the People Act, 1950 compilation dated 22 July 2025. It includes the 2021 amendments, four qualifying dates in section 14(b) and the Aadhaar-related provisions in sections 23(4)–(6). Read the compilation.

Later changes still matter

A dated compilation does not establish the absence of later amendments. The applicable Act, commencement instructions and rule versions must be read together.

The declaration question

Section 31 concerns a written false statement with the specified knowledge or belief. It does not establish that every previously registered person applying again commits an offence, or settle the wording of a later online form.
