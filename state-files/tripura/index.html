The further question is whether actual revision steps, notifications, Form 6 submissions and decision registers followed the operative schedule.

A later timetable must be distinguished

Akashvani reported deferral and a new qualifying date. Keep this broadcaster account separate from the original signed May schedule. Do not present either as a verified current deadline without the replacement instrument.
