CASE STUDIES / TESTING THE SAFEGUARDSBihar

The judgment and the individual decision

The Bihar judgment establishes the legal starting point. The next task is to distinguish the framework the Court upheld from the correctness of a particular local decision.

THE QUESTION

What follows from upholding the SIR framework?

How the record developed

  1. 01

    The framework is challenged

    The national debate begins with the intensive revision and its relationship to election law, eligibility and access to remedies.

  2. 02

    The Court decides the framework

    The May judgment upholds the broad process while placing legal bounds on citizenship-related determinations.

  3. 03

    Implementation remains a separate test

    A ruling on the framework does not decide every subsequent notice, form version, hearing or exclusion. Those require their own records.

WHAT THE RECORD SUPPORTS

What is established

The archive contains the May judgment and its legal qualifications.

THE LIMIT

Where the evidence stops

The holding does not certify every local decision or later implementation.

What remains unresolved

Case-level notices, hearings, decisions, citizenship referrals and competent-authority outcomes.

A documentary finding and an individual outcome are different. We keep the missing evidence visible.
