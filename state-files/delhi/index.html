ARCHIVE STATUSActive

Last desk review: 15 October 2026

A large verification exercise under a changing timetable

Delhi entered the May 2026 revision with a 2002 historical-roll reference. The Commission’s September update extended the claims-and-objections deadline to 30 October. Reports describe considerable document verification in cases where a historical link was missing or logically inconsistent. Verified cases, accepted inclusions and retained entries are not equivalent measures.

What the original ECI schedule said

Relevant historical reference01 Jan 2002
Original draft target05 Aug 2026
Original final publication target07 Oct 2026

These are original Annexures V and VI, not proof that a later amended schedule did not supersede them.

The baseline is a reference, not a verdict

Being unlinked to the January 2002 roll is not proof of non-citizenship or absence of ordinary residence in 2026. The legal question for the ERO remains present eligibility.

The response to a notice must count

The number of people who supplied documents is an activity measure. What matters for inclusion is how many decisions were made, how many were favourable and whether the correct entries appeared in the final roll.

A practical route to accountability

Data should be obtainable by constituency: flagged cases, served notices, responses received, reasons for rejection, successful appeals and corrections.

What this file still needs

  1. How many discrepancy notices were disposed of before the final roll?
  2. How many eligible electors were successfully restored following follow-up visits?
  3. Were all claims-period extensions reflected in accessible official notices?
Evidence classification: This case contains independently locatable official documents and some unresolved reported or case-level issues. Each paragraph distinguishes legal conclusion from unanswered administrative fact.
